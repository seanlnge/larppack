# ScentNode (general)

## One-line pitch

ScentNode helps people turn ordinary mail and packages into memorable sensory experiences through scent-enhanced packaging.

## Product summary

ScentNode is a personalized olfactory packaging product designed for anyone sending letters, gifts, packages, or online orders. It adds a subtle scent layer to packaging through scented inserts, cards, or materials that make opening a package feel warmer, more emotional, and more memorable. Instead of generic mail, ScentNode helps people create packaging experiences tied to mood, emotion, memory, or personal identity.

## Target audience

* People sending gifts to friends, partners, or family
* Online sellers wanting more memorable packages
* Creators and artists shipping products or prints
* Long-distance couples and pen-pal communities
* Anyone who wants packages to feel more personal and emotional

## Core value props

* Makes packages feel more thoughtful and memorable
* Adds emotional depth to gifting and personal mail
* Creates stronger memory associations through scent
* Simple way to personalize packages without expensive customization
* Turns ordinary unboxing into a sensory experience

## Brand voice

* Warm and emotionally driven
* Modern and intimate
* Creative and atmospheric
* Personal without sounding cheesy

## Visual direction

* Soft cinematic photography of letters, boxes, and gift wrapping
* Warm lighting and nostalgic tones
* Close-up tactile textures like paper, ribbon, wax seals, and packaging materials
* Cozy, intimate, emotionally resonant scenes
* Minimal but premium presentation

## CTA direction

* Encourage users to make their packages unforgettable
* Emphasize emotional memory and sensory experience
* Mention website naturally in final CTA
* Keep CTA warm, personal, and action-oriented
