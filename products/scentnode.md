# ScentNode

## One-line pitch

ScentNode helps e-commerce brands create memorable unboxing experiences through custom scent-enhanced packaging.

## Product summary

ScentNode is a sensory branding product focused on the olfactory side of product packaging and unboxing. It helps brands create scent experiences that make packages feel more premium, emotionally memorable, and aligned with their identity. Instead of generic packaging, brands receive custom scent recommendations and scented packaging inserts designed to reinforce brand perception during the first physical customer interaction.

## Target audience

* Shopify and direct-to-consumer brands
* Indie fragrance, candle, skincare, and lifestyle brands
* Brands focused on premium presentation and customer experience
* E-commerce sellers looking to differentiate their unboxing experience

## Core value props

* Creates stronger emotional association with a brand
* Makes unboxing feel more premium and memorable
* Adds a sensory layer competitors usually ignore
* Simple implementation without requiring full packaging redesigns
* Custom scent recommendations tailored to product identity and brand positioning

## Brand voice

* Premium and modern
* Emotionally intelligent
* Sensory-focused and experiential
* Sophisticated without sounding corporate

## Visual direction

* Cinematic luxury product photography
* Minimalist packaging close-ups
* Soft lighting, shadows, textured materials
* Warm editorial aesthetics with subtle atmospheric haze
* Focus on tactile and sensory cues

## CTA direction

* Encourage brands to elevate their customer experience through scent
* Position scent as an overlooked competitive advantage
* Mention website naturally in final CTA
* Keep CTA direct, premium, and action-oriented
