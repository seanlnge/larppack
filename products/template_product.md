# ScentNode

## One-line pitch
ScentNode helps beginners design personalized fragrances without needing chemistry expertise.

## Product summary
ScentNode is a guided fragrance-creation product for first-time creators. It translates mood, memory, and personal taste into scent directions people can actually act on. Instead of technical overwhelm, users get clear guided steps, curated ingredient paths, and confidence-building prompts.

## Target audience
- Creative beginners curious about perfume-making
- Lifestyle creators who want a signature scent identity
- Gift shoppers who want a meaningful, custom-feeling fragrance experience

## Core value props
- Beginner-friendly guidance with plain-language prompts
- Fast path from idea to scent direction
- Creative confidence without lab-coat complexity
- Repeatable framework for refining scent preferences over time

## Brand voice
- Warm, modern, creative
- Premium but approachable
- Motivating, not intimidating

## Visual direction
- Editorial, cinematic, premium lifestyle photography
- Floral, intimate, atmospheric compositions
- Soft contrast, rich color, subtle luxury

## CTA direction
- Encourage the viewer to start their first scent journey
- Mention website naturally in final CTA
- Keep CTA specific and action-oriented
