# ClipForge



## One-line pitch

ClipForge automatically turns Twitch streams into viral-ready TikTok clips without needing an editor.



## Product summary

ClipForge is an AI-powered content repurposing platform for streamers and creators. It watches streams, detects high-energy moments, and automatically generates short-form clips optimized for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Instead of manually scrubbing through hours of footage, creators wake up to ready-to-post content with captions, smart framing, and viral-focused edits already applied.



## Target audience

- Small-to-mid-sized Twitch streamers trying to grow faster

- Creators who stream consistently but struggle with short-form content

- Solo creators who cannot afford editors or clipping teams

- VTubers, gaming creators, and reaction streamers



## Core value props

- Automatically detects hype, funny, emotional, and high-engagement moments

- Converts long streams into vertical short-form content automatically

- Removes the need for manual clipping and editing

- Helps creators stay consistent on TikTok and Shorts without extra work

- Designed to maximize discoverability and audience growth



## Brand voice

- Energetic, internet-native, creator-focused

- Confident and growth-oriented

- Smart but simple

- Feels like a powerful creator tool, not enterprise software
